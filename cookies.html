What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in much the same way. They can be essential to a site working, or used for analytics and advertising.

What we use

We keep our use to a minimum. This website currently uses no analytics or advertising cookies, and our fonts and images are served from our own site rather than from third parties.

Managing your preferences

When you first visit, a short banner at the foot of the screen asks for your choice. Once you choose, it will not appear again on that device.

You can review or change your choices at any time using Cookie Settings, which also appears at the foot of every page. You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings, although some features may then not work as intended.

Our clients' websites

When we set up advertising systems for clients, tracking technologies such as the Meta Pixel may be used on the client's own website. Those are governed by the client's cookie policy and consent tools, not this one.

Updates

We will update this policy if our use of cookies changes. Questions can be sent to enquire@yvishausens.com.
