Explore the System

A closer look at the Meta advertising system we build, run and refine for our clients: how it is set up, how it learns, and how proven work is scaled and kept. Eight parts, one system.

01 — FOUNDATION

Tracking you can trust.

Every decision the system makes is only as good as the signal it receives.

Before a single ad runs, we make sure Meta can see what actually happens on your website. Events are sent twice, from the visitor’s browser and from your server, then matched and de-duplicated so nothing is lost and nothing is counted twice.

  • ACCOUNTSBusiness Manager, ad account, pages and permissions structured correctly, with you as owner.
  • PIXELBrowser events: page views, product views, add to cart, checkout and purchase.
  • CONVERSIONS APIThe same events sent server-side, so signal survives ad blockers and browser limits.
  • VERIFICATIONDomain verification, event priorities and match quality checked before launch.

02 — AUDIENCES

From everyone, to your people.

Audiences are built in layers, each warmer and more valuable than the last.

Meta’s delivery is at its best when given room to find buyers. We combine broad prospecting with audiences modelled on your best customers, and keep the people who already know you in their own layer, so each is spoken to differently and never over-served.

  • BROADOpen prospecting that lets the algorithm find new buyers at scale.
  • LOOKALIKESAudiences modelled on your purchasers and highest-value customers.
  • ENGAGEDVisitors, viewers and followers who have shown interest.
  • CUSTOMERSPast buyers, handled with care: excluded, or invited back.

03 — CREATIVE

Test the idea, not the guess.

Creative is the largest lever in modern Meta advertising, so it is tested with discipline.

We test one variable at a time: the hook, the format, the angle, the offer. Each variant gets a fair budget and a fair window, and is judged on the metrics that matter at its stage, from whether it stops the scroll to whether it sells.

  • HOOKSThe first seconds and the opening line, tested side by side.
  • FORMATSStatic, video, carousel, feed and story placements.
  • ANGLESDifferent reasons to buy, spoken to different people.
  • SIGNALSHook rate, click-through, cost per result and conversion rate.

04 — STRUCTURE

A place for everything.

Testing, scaling and retargeting each run in their own space, so one never disrupts another.

New ideas are proven in a controlled testing environment. Only what earns it graduates into scaling, where budget is concentrated. Warm audiences are handled separately again. Budget flows towards evidence, never away from it.

  • TESTControlled budgets, clear hypotheses, fixed evaluation windows.
  • SCALEProven winners only, with budget concentrated where it performs.
  • RETARGETWarm audiences, with messages matched to where they left off.

05 — OPTIMISE

Watched, not left.

Performance is monitored continuously, and every change has a reason behind it.

We watch cost per result, return on ad spend, frequency and creative fatigue against the targets agreed with you. When something drifts, we act: pausing what has stopped working, iterating on what is close, and backing what is performing.

  • PAUSEAds and audiences that no longer earn their spend.
  • ITERATENew versions of promising creative, before fatigue sets in.
  • SCALEMore budget behind what is consistently beating target.

06 — SCALE

Grow what is proven.

Scaling is where most accounts break. Ours is measured, and always within budgets you approve.

We scale in two directions. Vertically, by raising budgets on proven campaigns in measured steps, so delivery stays stable. Horizontally, by carrying winning creative into new audiences, placements and markets.

  • VERTICALBudget increased in controlled steps, with performance checked at each one.
  • HORIZONTALProven ads extended to new audiences, placements and regions.
  • GUARDRAILSSpend limits and approvals agreed with you before scaling begins.

07 — RECONNECT

Bring them back.

Most people do not buy on their first visit. The system remembers them.

Visitors who viewed a product or left a basket are reached again with messages that match where they stopped: a reminder, a reassurance, a reason to return. Frequency is capped so it stays welcome, never intrusive.

  • VIEWEDA reminder of what caught their eye.
  • ADDED TO CARTReassurance: delivery, returns, reviews, the finer details.
  • PURCHASEDExcluded from acquisition, or invited back at the right time.

08 — REPORT & ARCHIVE

Clear reports. Lasting knowledge.

You always know what is happening, why, and what we recommend next.

Regular reports translate the numbers into plain language and clear recommendations. Every winning hook, frame and edit is catalogued in your archive, so each new campaign starts from what has already been proven, not from zero.

  • REPORTSPerformance, spend and results against target, with recommendations.
  • INSIGHTSWhat worked, what did not, and why, written down.
  • ARCHIVEA growing library of proven creative and learnings, owned by you.
