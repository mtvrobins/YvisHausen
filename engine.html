01 — AUDIT

Understand before you spend.

Every programme begins with a strategic audit, not with ads.

Before anything is built, we study the whole picture: how ready your website is to convert, what your competitors are running and where the gaps are, and what your account history already tells us. From this we shape a funnel strategy built around your business, not a one-size-fits-all playbook.

  • WEBSITEYour website and landing pages reviewed for conversion readiness, with clear recommendations.
  • COMPETITORSWhat your competitors are running, researched through the Meta Ad Library, and where the gaps are.
  • ACCOUNTExisting campaigns, tracking and set up reviewed for what has and has not worked.
  • FUNNEL STRATEGYA tailored plan from first impression to repeat purchase.

02 — SET UP & TRACKING

Built on clear signal.

The system can only learn from what it can see, so tracking comes first.*2

We set up and configure your Meta foundations inside accounts you own: Business Portfolio, ad account, Facebook Page and Instagram, with permissions structured correctly. Then the tracking: the Meta Pixel in the browser and the Conversions API on the server, for more accurate, privacy-resilient signal, with the events that matter to your business configured and tested.

  • ACCOUNTSBusiness Portfolio, ad account, Page and Instagram set up and connected, with you as owner.
  • META PIXELFull Pixel integration, configured and tested for your account.
  • CONVERSIONS APIServer-side tracking set up where your website platform supports it.*2
  • CUSTOM EVENTSButton clicks, form submissions, purchases and more, mapped to what matters for your business.
  • FRAMEWORKSUTM parameters and naming conventions, so results can be traced to their source.

03 — AUDIENCES

From everyone, to your people.

Audiences are designed in layers, each warmer and more valuable than the last.

It begins with your buyer. We build precise buyer personas from demographic, geographic and interest data, then design an audience architecture around them: broad prospecting that gives Meta room to find buyers, Lookalikes that mirror your best customers, and Custom Audiences of the people who already know you.

  • PERSONASBuyer personas built from demographic, geographic and interest data.
  • BROADOpen prospecting that gives Meta’s delivery room to find new buyers.
  • LOOKALIKESNew prospects who mirror your best customers.
  • CUSTOMWebsite visitors, engagers and your existing customer lists.*4
  • CUSTOMERSPast buyers handled with care: excluded from prospecting, or invited back.

04 — STRUCTURE

A place for everything.

Your campaign architecture is mapped around your goals, not a template.

Before launch, the full architecture is designed: which campaigns serve awareness, leads and sales, how testing, scaling and retargeting are kept apart, and how budget could be weighted between them. It is documented with naming conventions and templates, and your team is trained to run it with confidence.*5

  • ARCHITECTUREAwareness, lead and sales campaigns, each with a clear role.
  • TESTA controlled space for new ideas, with clear hypotheses and evaluation windows.
  • SCALEA separate space for what has proven itself.
  • RETARGETWarm audiences, reached with messages matched to where they left off.
  • TRAININGOptional hands-on training and knowledge transfer for a nominated team member, so your business retains the capability to understand and operate the system independently if required.*1

05 — CREATIVE STRATEGY

Test the idea, not the guess.

Creative is the largest lever in Meta advertising, so it is tested with a framework, not by instinct.

We design A/B testing frameworks for your headlines, hooks, angles and formats, one variable at a time, each judged on the signals that matter at its stage. Alongside, we give messaging and positioning guidance: we shape the strategy behind what converts; you own and approve every word that goes live.

  • FRAMEWORKSA/B tests planned for headlines, angles and formats.
  • MESSAGINGPositioning and messaging guidance for your ad copy and creative.
  • SPECIFICATIONSCreative specifications for each format and placement.
  • SIGNALSHook rate, click-through, cost per result and conversion rate, read in context.
  • YOUR APPROVALYour team produces, approves and publishes every ad.*5

06 — FUNNEL

Every stage, working together.

Cold traffic, retargeting and existing customers each need their own role and their own message.

Many accounts only chase new customers. Your funnel strategy maps how people move from first impression to repeat purchase: prospecting at the top, retargeting in the middle, and retention at the bottom, where lifetime value is built. Each stage feeds the next, and each is measured on what it is there to do.

  • TOP OF FUNNELCold traffic: new audiences meeting your brand for the first time.
  • MIDDLERetargeting: people who engaged, visited or added to cart.
  • BOTTOMExisting customers: retention, repeat purchase and lifetime value.
  • FULL BUILDSFully built multi-stage funnels are available through Advanced Support.*10

07 — REVIEW

Reviewed, then refined.

Regular performance reviews, with clear, actionable recommendations: not just numbers, but what to do with them.

We review your campaigns, audiences, creative and tracking against the goals agreed with you. You receive recommendations on which campaigns to scale and which to rework, backed by the data, with budget pacing guidance to help you avoid early-month overspend. Your team decides what to act on, and we talk it through together on your monthly strategy call.*7

  • REVIEWSCampaign, audience, creative and tracking reviews at regular intervals.*1
  • RECOMMENDATIONSWhat to scale, what to rework and what to test next, backed by the data.
  • PACINGBudget pacing guidance to help avoid early-month overspend.*6
  • TROUBLESHOOTINGHelp diagnosing what is not working as expected.*3

08 — SCALE

Grow what is proven.

Scaling is where many accounts break, so it is planned in measured steps, and every spend decision stays yours.*6

When something is working, we recommend how to grow it. Vertically, by raising budget on proven campaigns in measured steps, so delivery stays stable. Horizontally, by carrying winning creative into new audiences, placements and markets. You set the budget and approve every step.

  • VERTICALRecommendations for raising budget in controlled steps, reviewed at each one.
  • HORIZONTALProven ads carried into new audiences, placements and regions.
  • ROADMAPA testing and scaling roadmap for the quarter ahead.
  • YOUR DECISIONBudgets, limits and approvals always stay with you.

09 — RECONNECT

Bring them back.

Most people do not buy on their first visit. A well-designed system remembers them.

We design retargeting journeys for people who viewed a product or left a basket, with messages matched to where they stopped: a reminder, a reassurance, a reason to return. Frequency is kept in check so it stays welcome, never intrusive.

  • VIEWEDA reminder of what caught their eye.
  • ADDED TO CARTReassurance: delivery, returns, reviews, the finer details.
  • PURCHASEDExcluded from prospecting, or invited back at the right time.

10 — REPORTING

Clear reports. Clear next moves.

You know what is happening, why, and what we recommend next.

Each month you receive a clear performance breakdown, as a written report or a recorded video walkthrough,*8 with strategic direction for the month ahead: what to test, what to pivot, and where to double down. Learnings are documented as they build, so each new campaign starts from what has already been learned, not from zero.

  • MONTHLY BREAKDOWNPerformance, spend and results against the goals agreed with you.
  • STRATEGY CALLA 60-minute strategy call each month to talk it through.*7
  • DIRECTIONWhat to test, pivot or double down on next month.
  • LEARNINGSInsights documented in your reports, so progress builds over time.
