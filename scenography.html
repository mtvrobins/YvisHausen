Scenography

CREATIVE HOUSE

Spatial worlds for fashion, film and exhibition.

Yvis Hausen designs sets and environments for fashion presentations, film production, exhibitions and cultural experiences — spaces built to carry a narrative.

Our approach treats scenography as storytelling in three dimensions, shaping how an audience moves through and experiences a moment.

Work is developed privately, in collaboration with directors, curators and brand teams.
