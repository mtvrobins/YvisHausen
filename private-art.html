Art & Cultural Advisory

ART & CULTURAL

Yvis Hausen is developing a private art advisory practice focused on the discovery, sourcing and placement of exceptional works across contemporary art and visual culture.

As our other work in the Creative House sector increasingly intersected with artists, collectors, designers and cultural institutions, we began developing a dedicated Art & Cultural Advisory practice. Our approach is intentionally selective, connecting artists, collectors, creative institutions and luxury environments through considered introductions and bespoke sourcing.

We are currently developing relationships with artists, galleries, collectors and private clients ahead of the formal launch of the practice.
