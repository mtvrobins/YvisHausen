Sound Architecture

CREATIVE HOUSE

Composition as a spatial and sensory language.

Yvis Hausen creates original sound for fashion, film and immersive environments — work built to be felt as much as heard, shaping mood, pace and memory within a space or a story.

Our compositions are developed specifically for runway, screen and installation, translating a brand's identity or a director's vision into sonic form.

Current work is undertaken privately and by commission, in close collaboration with the creative teams behind each project.
