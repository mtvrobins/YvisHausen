ART & CULTURAL

To Artists,

Selective relationships, thoughtful placements.

We are interested in artists with a distinctive visual language, considered practice and a strong individual point of view.

Our approach is intentionally selective. We build relationships with artists whose work we believe can create meaningful connections with the right collectors, spaces and cultural environments.

If you are an artist or represent an artist whose practice you believe may align with Yvis Hausen, we invite you to introduce your work to our team.

Please include your portfolio, website or Instagram, a short biography, current representation (if applicable), and any relevant information regarding available works.

All submissions are reviewed privately.
