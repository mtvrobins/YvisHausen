# System Page review against the contract — 9 Oct 2026 (to revisit)

Waiting on: the new contract and the final 90-day programme scope.
Raise this list when the System Page / contract topic comes back up.

## Contradictions with the contract
1. Training: SP 05 says "Optional"; Programme, Responsibilities and Schedule 1A make Month 1 client training core. Decide core vs Advanced Support, then align.
2. Passwords: SP says "We will never ask for your password"; clause 5.4 only says "where Meta's permission systems provide an alternative". Keep the SP wording, tighten 5.4.
3. Minimum terms: "ONGOING — Monthly support" reads as rolling; contract is 3 months then an automatic 6-month Secondary Term at £1,200. Add a note: "Programmes have a minimum term, set out in your proposal and agreement."
4. Reporting: SP says "Each month you receive a clear performance breakdown"; reporting is non-core (Schedule 2, clause 3.8). Make it core in the contract, or soften the SP.
5. Audit: "Every programme begins with a strategic audit" and the website review are stated as certain; competitor research is non-core and the website review is only Month 2. Soften ("where relevant") or make core.

## Wording that oversells
6. Reconnect: "never intrusive" → "We recommend frequency limits so it stays welcome, not intrusive."
7. Scale: "Proven ads carried into new audiences" → "Recommendations for carrying proven ads…".
8. "Precise buyer personas" → "detailed"; "Creative is the largest lever" → "often the largest".

## Missing
9. Cookie consent: add to note *2 — "Your website's cookie consent and privacy notice remain your responsibility."

## Marketing page
10. "We become a part of your team / an extension of your company" vs clause 26.2 (no partnership or agency): consider "We work alongside your team". "Engineered for compounding ROAS" edges towards a ROAS promise (clause 8): consider "built so every result informs the next".

## Readability for owners new to Meta
- Tap/hover glossary on jargon (Business Portfolio, Pixel, Conversions API, UTM, Lookalikes, Custom Audiences, hook rate, cost per result, attribution, vertical/horizontal scaling, LTV).
- One plain "in short" line under each of the 11 section headings.

Not legal advice: have a solicitor check the contract changes (points 2 and 4) before launch.
